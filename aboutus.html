A journey fueled by innovation, passion and leadership.

I’m Fidele, a Rwandan student growing through programming, robotics, creative problem-solving and youth leadership. From participating in national-level robotics competitions such as FLL to organizing technology events and building practical projects, each experience is helping me grow toward software engineering, applied artificial intelligence and creating technology initiatives that solve real challenges.

Robotics

My Robotics Journey

Robotics gave me the opportunity to move beyond learning concepts and apply them in real challenges. Through national-level competitions such as FLL , FTC and other team experiences, I learned how to design, test, adapt and communicate ideas while working toward a shared goal.

Leadership

Creating opportunities for others.

As I grew through technology and robotics, I began creating opportunities where students who share an interest in technology could bring together different skills, ideas and perspectives. Through the ICT and Innovation Club, I helped organize initiatives such as the Scratch Competition and AI for Good Week, giving young people space to learn, compete, collaborate and turn their ideas into practical solutions.
